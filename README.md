## 1. Overview & Abstract

Contemporary space science operates across an extraordinary dynamic range of spatial, temporal, and energetic scales, linking quantum-seeded fluctuations in the primordial plasma to the relativistic dynamics of supermassive compact objects, the demographic assembly of exoplanetary systems, and the engineering physics of interplanetary and interstellar transport.

This repository hosts the complete research manuscript and the interactive academic web application for **"Multiscale Astrophysics, Observational Cosmology, and Astrodynamic Architectures: A Unified Synthesis of Contemporary Space Science"** by **Samuelson G**. The study synthesizes empirical constraints and theoretical frameworks across four foundational domains:

1. **Relativistic Cosmology & Background Dynamics:** Full-mission cosmic microwave background (CMB) temperature ($TT$), polarization ($TE, EE, \text{lowE}$), and lensing reconstructions confirming the spatially flat six-parameter $\Lambda$CDM model at early epochs ($H_0 = 67.4 \pm 0.5\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$, $\Omega_m = 0.315 \pm 0.007$), contrasted against the $>5\sigma$ late-universe Hubble constant discrepancy ($H_0 = 73.04 \pm 1.04\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$) and quadratic-curvature effective field theory (EFT) perturbation extensions.
2. **Cosmic Dawn ($z > 14$) & Next-Generation Radio Cosmology:** Multi-observatory infrared and submillimeter characterization of the earliest ultraluminous galaxies—including **JADES-GS-z14-0** ($z = 14.1793 \pm 0.0007$, ~290 Myr post-Big Bang) and **MoM-z14** ($z = 14.44$, ~280 Myr post-Big Bang)—alongside neutral hydrogen ($21\text{ cm}$) intensity mapping system temperature models for Phase 1 of the Square Kilometre Array (**SKA1-MID**).
3. **Stellar Evolution & Exoplanetary Demographics:** Statistical audit of the **6,464 confirmed exoplanets** in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (including 1,008 confirmed TESS discoveries through late 2026), quantifying the observational selection dichotomy between transit photometry ($>74\%$ of detections) and the $51.73\%$ missingness bottleneck in dynamical planetary mass measurements ($n = 2,700$ mass-validated worlds).
4. **Astrodynamics & Spacecraft Propulsion Architectures:** Comparative engineering physics governed by the Tsiolkovsky rocket equation across cryogenic chemical ($I_{\text{sp}} \approx 440\text{ s}$), green nitrous oxide ($\text{N}_2\text{O}$), solid-core Nuclear Thermal Propulsion ($I_{\text{sp}} \approx 880\text{--}900\text{ s}$), electrostatic/electromagnetic ion and plasma drives (GIT, Hall, VASIMR, MOA; $I_{\text{sp}} \approx 1,000\text{--}39,800\text{ s}$), nuclear pulse propulsion (Orion class), and propellantless photonic solar sails ($v_e = c$).

## 2. Key Scientific Benchmarks

* **Early-Universe CMB Baseline (Planck):** $H_0 = 67.4 \pm 0.5\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$, $\Omega_m = 0.315 \pm 0.007$, $100\theta_* = 1.0411 \pm 0.0003$, $\sigma_8 = 0.811 \pm 0.006$.
* **Late-Universe Distance Ladder Probes:**
* **SH0ES (Cepheids + SNe Ia):** $H_0 = 73.04 \pm 1.04\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$ ($5.3\sigma$ tension when combined with H0LiCOW time-delay cosmography).
* **JAGB Carbon Stars + SNe Ia:** $H_0 = 73.3 \pm 1.4\text{ (stat)} \pm 2.0\text{ (sys)}\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$.
* **Type II Supernova SN 2023ixf Sample:** $H_0 = 73.1^{+3.68}_{-3.50}\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$.
* **Expanding Photosphere Method (EPM):** $H_0 = 72.9^{+5.7}_{-4.3}\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$.


* **Cosmic Dawn Spectroscopic Frontier:**
* **MoM-z14:** Redshift $z = 14.44$ (~280 Myr after the Big Bang).
* **JADES-GS-z14-0:** Spectroscopic redshift $z = 14.1793 \pm 0.0007$ (via ALMA ionized oxygen), physical half-light radius $260 \pm 20\text{ pc}$ (~1,600 ly luminous diameter), light travel distance $13.428\text{ Gly}$, and present proper comoving distance $33.844\text{ Gly}$.


* **Exoplanet Census (November 2026):** 6,464 confirmed exoplanets (1,008 TESS + 5,437 other facilities as of October 8, 2026; 361 added in 2026 including HIP 39330 b). Mass-characterized cohort ($n = 2,700$) exhibits a bimodal radial-velocity selection skew toward Jupiter-like ($32.93\%$, 889 planets) and Neptune-like ($24.78\%$, 669 planets) worlds versus Earth-like terrestrial worlds ($2.52\%$, 68 planets).

## 6. Synthesis and Conclusions

A unified evaluation of contemporary space science reveals that breakthroughs across cosmological, galactic, exoplanetary, and astrodynamic scales are tightly coupled by shared instrumentation and physical constraints:

1. **Cosmological Background & Expansion History:** At the macroscopic scale, the spatially flat base-$\Lambda$CDM model provides an extraordinary fit to early-universe CMB temperature, polarization, and lensing spectra ($100\theta_* = 1.0411 \pm 0.0003$, $H_0 = 67.4 \pm 0.5\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$) as well as intermediate-redshift BAO standards from BOSS DR11 and DESI DR1. However, the persistence of a $>5\sigma$ discrepancy against multiple independent late-universe calibrators—including Cepheids ($73.04 \pm 1.04\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$), JAGB carbon stars ($73.3 \pm 1.4\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$), Type II supernova SN 2023ixf ($73.1^{+3.68}_{-3.50}\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$), and Expanding Photosphere Method distances ($72.9^{+5.7}_{-4.3}\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{ Mpc}^{-1}$)—demonstrates that simple single-parameter extensions ($\Omega_k$, $w$, $N_{\text{eff}}$) cannot reconcile the cosmic expansion history. Future resolution depends on sub-arcsecond $21\text{ cm}$ neutral hydrogen intensity mapping with SKA1-MID across intermediate redshifts and precision growth-of-structure tests capable of isolating quadratic-curvature EFT suppressions ($0.66\%$ in $f\sigma_8$ at $z = 0$) or gigaparsec-scale local void outflows.
2. **Rapid Baryonic Assembly at Cosmic Dawn ($z > 14$):** The spectroscopic confirmation of **JADES-GS-z14-0** ($z = 14.1793 \pm 0.0007$) via JWST and ALMA, alongside the discovery of **MoM-z14** ($z = 14.44$, $280\text{ Myr}$ post-Big Bang), proves that spatially extended ($260\text{ pc}$ half-light radius), oxygen-enriched galaxies assembled far earlier than predicted by standard constant-efficiency dark-matter halo models. Deep mid-infrared ($7.7\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ JWST/MIRI) and submillimeter (ALMA) synergy is essential to disentangle early stellar feedback and Population III-to-II transitions during the first $200\text{--}250\text{ Myr}$ of cosmic time.
3. **Exoplanetary Demographics & The Mass Bottleneck:** Within the Galactic domain, while all-sky space transit surveys have propelled the confirmed exoplanet catalog to $6,464$ worlds (including $1,008$ confirmed TESS discoveries), the $51.73\%$ missingness deficit in dynamical planetary mass measurements represents the primary empirical bottleneck in comparative planetology. Closing this gap requires pairing dedicated space-based stellar-activity calibration missions such as NASA's Pandora small satellite (launched January 2026, science operations August 2026) with extreme-precision radial-velocity follow-up to populate the terrestrial ($1\text{--}2\text{ }M_{\text{Earth}}$) mass-radius regime without relying on biased statistical mean imputation.
4. **Bifurcated Astrodynamic Propulsion Strategy:** Because conventional cryogenic chemical propulsion is fundamentally bounded near $I_{\text{sp}} \approx 440\text{ s}$ ($v_e \approx 4.31\text{ km s}^{-1}$), sustainable expansion across cislunar, interplanetary, and interstellar space requires a bifurcated engineering architecture: deploying green chemical thrusters ($\text{N}_2\text{O}$) and solar/nuclear electric drives (GIT, Hall, VASIMR, MOA; $I_{\text{sp}} \approx 1,000\text{--}39,800\text{ s}$) for orbital station-keeping, In-Space Servicing, Assembly, and Manufacturing (ISAM), and outer-planet cargo tugs, while advancing solid-core Nuclear Thermal Propulsion ($I_{\text{sp}} \approx 880\text{--}900\text{ s}$) and perihelion-boosted photonic solar sails ($908\text{ N km}^{-2}$ at $0.1\text{ AU}$) for rapid crewed Mars transits and interstellar precursor missions.
